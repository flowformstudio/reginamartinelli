Why the Self-Improvement Industry Has It Wrong

After over two decades in this work, I’ve watched the self-improvement industry take 3 exhausting approaches to transformation that just don’t work:

1) There’s a Better Version of You: The idea of our “Best Self” implies who you are isn’t enough. It has us exhausting ourselves trying to prove our worth. Yet, we were born Enough. This sham of a “Best Self” only works because we believe something’s wrong with us. There isn’t. Every version of you is equally worthy, precious & beautiful. The real work isn’t changing who you are. It’s seeing who you’ve always been.

2) The Force-and-Willpower Route: It’s about overpowering your procrastination, battling yourself into making changes or using willpower to force yourself into action. It feels exhausting, and when you can’t maintain that level consistently, it’s a setup for the guilt and shame of failing.

3) The Light-Touch Route: Meditation apps, podcast insights, journal affirmations, and positive thinking are great for a feel-good, endorphin weekend, but these do not go deep enough to activate real, lasting change in your nervous system.

Here’s where they miss the boat: Old survival patterns that keep us spinning are like a computer’s software in our brain signaling anxiety, depression, confusion, and loneliness… all of which would have anyone wanting to crawl out of their own skin.

What they don’t understand is that real transformation makes you a foundation, not just someone with better coping skills. You become the person others instinctively trust, not because you have all the answers, but because you’ve found your center and nothing can throw you off.

Magic happens when you transform the old survival strategies into ones that support what you want, and where you want to go now.
