My Story

A Journey Back to Myself

I come from five generations of farmers. Started working the fields at age six. “Work hard” wasn’t advice in my family—it was survival. So when good things came too easily my nervous system hit the panic button. “Who am I to have it easier than my ancestors who struggled so much?”

I tried everything to break this pattern. I read every self-help book. Got certified in everything—Master’s in Change Management, transformational NLP, Family Constellations. Led seminars. Coached others. But I had strong internal guard rails limiting my happiness. It felt like being on a short leash and being yanked back whenever life got “too good.”

Here’s what I discovered… Our personal guard rails get installed before we turn six.

In those early years, we decide how much love we deserve, how safe we are, how much space we can take up and how much struggle we deserve.

And if your childhood was unstable or wounded… then your nervous system installs safety locks or guard rails to protect you.

The Problem: Thirty years later, those same “protective guard rails” are suffocating your joy, creativity, certainty, ease and freedom.

What Changed

I updated her software.

I finally stopped trying to think my way out. Stopped battling myself with willpower. Stopped slapping affirmations over deep wounds.

Instead, I went to the source, that little girl who learned she was “too much” and “not enough” at the same time. Who had to read every room, manage everyone’s emotions, and work herself to exhaustion just to belong.

I didn’t fight or shame her. I updated her software.

My Life Today…

Quiet, unforced, mine.

  • My grape buyer went bankrupt, threatening everything I’d built. Yet I sleep like a baby.
  • I say no without the three-day guilt hangover.
  • My six-year-old gets the present mama he deserves, not the anxious ghost I used to be.
  • Money flows because I finally feel worthy of receiving it.

This isn’t about becoming someone new. It’s about remembering who you’ve always been underneath all that programming and personal guard rails.
