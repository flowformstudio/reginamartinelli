IV My Story

I know this because I lived the alternative.

For a long time, I was marketing the way I thought I was supposed to.

I was following what looked like the “proven” method. Trying to make my business fit the models that seemed to work for other people.

But the more I did that, the less like myself my business felt.

So I stepped back.

I took two years off and went deep into my own research, inner work, and truth.

What I found was I had…

  • Fears to unravel. “I don’t know how to do this differently in the way that feels good to me. Where do I even start?”
  • Conditioning to question. “The smart thing to do is follow the proven formula, even if it doesn’t feel like me.”
  • Beliefs to revise. “It’s never really been safe to fully show up as me.”
  • Shame to dismantle. “I will look stupid.”

And underneath all of it was a deeper question:

Do I own the whole of who I am, the beliefs I have, the work I love doing and doing it in the way I want?

…Or do I keep approaching my business from dread, hoping one day something will change for me?

I was clear I wanted to work with dream clients, make a real impact, have more spaciousness in my week, raise my son, enjoy my life, and create success in a balanced and sustainable way.

Yet I was raised in a farming family that deeply values hard work, sacrifice and struggle. (I started working in the fields at 6 yrs old.)

So I had to question the idea that “struggle made me more worthy.”

I had to let go of the shame of wanting it to feel easier, more spacious, joyful and balanced.

And as I did, something became very clear:

When I began building my business around my natural strengths, gifts, talents, and the way I genuinely love to support people the alignment was profound.

And one of the tools that helped me see this most clearly was Human Design.

Not as a label. Not as a box. But as a deeply validating blueprint that gave me permission to be how I actually am — and to work in ways that genuinely support me.

It helped me understand that I hadn’t failed. I had simply been trying to build in ways that were never natural for me.

After decades of trying to do work, productivity, and proving, that was a profound shift.

It gave me language for how I best use my energy, how I’m meant to make decisions, how I naturally communicate, ideal ways to recharge for my specific wiring, and why certain business models or marketing methods felt so draining.

Suddenly, my past struggles made sense.

The more I leaned into my truth, the more aligned everything became: the joy in my business, the spaciousness in my personal life, and my financial success.

This is what I want for You.

I recently worked with a client who was beyond busy.

She was constantly networking, always posting, endlessly trying to bring people in. And still, her business felt unclear, scattered, and far more effortful than it should.

When I asked her what she helps her clients with, she listed four big, meaningful things:

  • Self-Expression
  • Self-Love
  • Worthiness
  • Abundance

All beautiful. All important. But not the same message.

They are not the same doorway.

They do not speak to the same person in the same way.

And they do not create the kind of clarity that helps someone instantly know, “Yes, this is for me!”

Her messaging is trying to hold too much, causing the marketing to feel broad, unclear, and diluted.

She ended up attracting clients who push back, who are challenging, and who demanded a lot of her. Her work stopped feeling satisfying because she was constantly shape-shifting to meet their needs, instead of standing in her own strengths with boundaries.

So I asked her a different question:
“What do you love helping your clients with most?”

She said: “Self-expression.”

This is not just a topic… its a Strength.

It was the clearest expression of where her work had power.

It was the thread that made the rest make sense.

When she saw this, we sharpened her message with clarity. Her marketing became more focused. Her ideal clients could recognize themselves in her work. And she stopped spending so much energy trying to be everything to everyone.

She began getting opportunities where she was paid more and doing less. More of her dream-style clients started showing up and it felt effortless.
